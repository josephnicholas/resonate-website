# Resonate Music Therapy Studio Website

Marketing/informational website for Resonate Music Therapy Studio, a music therapy practice in Dumaguete City, Negros Oriental — the only one in the Visayas region.

## Language

**Music Therapy**:
"A clinical and evidence-based application of music interventions to accomplish individualized goals within the therapeutic relationship by a credentialed professional who has completed an approved music therapy program" (American Music Therapy Association). The studio's core clinical service line.
_Avoid_: Music lessons, music class — those describe Adaptive Music Lessons, a distinct non-clinical offering.

**Individual Therapy**:
One-on-one music therapy sessions tailored to a single client's specific needs and goals.

**Group Therapy**:
Music therapy sessions for neurodivergent children focused on improving social skills, run with at least three clients per session.
_Avoid_: Group lessons.

**Adaptive Music Lessons**:
Voice, piano, and guitar lessons adapted to an individual's abilities and needs. Instructional, not clinical therapy.
_Avoid_: Music therapy — this is lessons, not a therapeutic intervention.

**Company Team Building Activities**:
Group activities for corporate clients aimed at improving team communication, collaboration, and morale. The studio's non-clinical, B2B offering.

**Tempo Tunes**:
A named 10-day music enrichment program for babies, toddlers, and preschoolers, distinct from the studio's individual/group therapy services. Play-based, uses live music.
_Avoid_: Music therapy program — Tempo Tunes is enrichment, not clinical therapy.

**Who We Serve**:
The populations the studio serves — individuals and groups of all ages and abilities, neurodivergent children, companies, and babies/toddlers/preschoolers (via Tempo Tunes). Distinct from "Services," which describes the offerings themselves rather than who receives them.
